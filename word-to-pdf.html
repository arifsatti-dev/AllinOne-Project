About this tool

Turn DOC and DOCX files into clean PDFs that open the same on every device. This converter uses a conversion service connected to the website. Connect your backend in assets/config.js to enable it.

How to use it

  1. Drop your Word file in the box.
  2. Wait while it converts.
  3. Your PDF downloads automatically.
