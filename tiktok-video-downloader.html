About this tool

Paste a TikTok link to get the video file for offline use. Perfect for backing up your own TikToks. Please respect creators and only save videos you have permission to download.

How to use it

  1. Tap Share on the TikTok video and copy the link.
  2. Paste it in the box above.
  3. Press Fetch and download.
