About this tool

Reduce the size of your pictures without a visible loss in quality. Everything happens inside your browser, so your photos are never uploaded to any server. Ideal for websites, email attachments and forms with file-size limits.

How to use it

  1. Drop one or many images into the box.
  2. Choose the quality and maximum width.
  3. Download each compressed image.

Yes, select or drop as many as you like.
