About this tool

Combine multiple PDF files into a single file in the order you choose. Merging happens in your browser, so confidential documents never leave your computer.

How to use it

  1. Add two or more PDF files.
  2. Arrange them in the order you want.
  3. Press Merge PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a page limit?

No fixed limit; very large files depend on your device.

Are password-protected PDFs supported?

Remove the password first, then merge.

Is it private?

Yes, files are processed locally.
