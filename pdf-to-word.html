About this tool

Turn PDF files into editable Word documents. This converter uses a secure conversion service connected to the website. Connect your backend in assets/config.js to enable it.

How to use it

  1. Drop your PDF in the box.
  2. Wait while it converts.
  3. Your DOCX file downloads automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Will the layout stay the same?

Simple documents convert very well; complex layouts may need small fixes.

Can it read scanned PDFs?

Only if your conversion service includes OCR.

Is it free?

Yes.
